Why This Dataset?

Three clear reasons drove this choice over other rainfall datasets:

1
Statistical Power: 4,090 records over 115 years provide large enough sample sizes for robust Bayesian computation, distribution fitting, and polynomial regression without overfitting.
2
Spatial Diversity: 36 meteorological subdivisions span Rajasthan desert (62 mm/yr) to Kerala coast (6,300+ mm/yr), enabling cross-region independence tests and distribution comparisons.
3
Official Authority: IMD is India's nodal government agency for meteorology. Its records carry standardised instrument calibration and quality control absent from citizen or satellite-derived alternatives.

Descriptive Statistics · Annual Rainfall

Summary Statistics (Annual Precipitation)

IMD Sub-divisional Dataset · N = 4,090 records

Annual rainfall is highly right-skewed (mean 1,411 mm vs median 1,121 mm), reflecting the large positive outliers in extremely wet subdivisions like the Andaman Islands and Kerala coast. The IQR of 840 mm confirms wide regional spread.
